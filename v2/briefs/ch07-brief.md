# Brief ch07 — "Source literacy for agent builders" (autopsy before belief)

Status: PROPOSED — batch approval with ch08–ch11. Frozen on approval.

## Outcome promise

Grade any AI-productivity claim into a written verdict (four buckets,
tier + date attached) before repeating or acting on it — and keep a
mini-ledger of your own.

## Coverage dimensions (matrix-bound)

METR RCT + walk-back (●) · Copilot-55% scrutiny (●) · benchmark
leakage/contamination/governance (●) · verdict vocabulary (●) ·
ledger habit (●).

## Venue

Scratch repo `claim-lab` (`CLAIM.md` + `EVIDENCE.md` + `LEDGER.md`,
≤3 files). Executed: staged claim restated, five-wound check,
four-bucket sort with reader pre-commit, mini-ledger banked. Agent
never fetches credentials; reader opens URLs in browser. Fully
book-only.

## Briefing (≤500w)

Coroner's five wounds (control? measurer? funding? contamination?
scope?) → same question opposite rigor (METR RCT: felt +20%,
measured −19%, public data, dated own doubt; vs Copilot 55%: n=95,
self-timed, no control, vendor-funded) → benchmarks-as-marketing
(leakage, contamination, governance, recall-vs-skill) → verdict
vocabulary (earn-belief / fails-scrutiny / wrong-frame /
insufficient-alone) + tiers → transfer: anything your agent asserts
gets claim + source + tier + date. Breaks-down: the checklist
filters bad evidence, doesn't manufacture certainty.

## Session (4 steps)

1. Stage the claim: restate ≤25 words, name measurer + falsifier,
   no edits.
2. Five-wound check: five lines, each ending PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN
   with quoted backing.
3. Four-bucket sort: reader pre-commits placements on paper first,
   then agent places all cards.
4. Bank the mini-ledger: URL · claim · tier · as-of · fetched?;
   STALE flagged.
