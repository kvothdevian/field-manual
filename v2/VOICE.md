# v2/VOICE.md — Style-B voice constraints (binding on every chapter)

Loaded into every writer alongside the brief and registry. Violations are
validator errors or adversary findings, never style suggestions.

## What the voice does

- Opens with a human stakes sentence (what you will be able to DO), never
  a thesis statement or a definition.
- One idea per section; each section earns its place or is cut.
- Concrete before abstract: the runnable session precedes the
  generalization, and the generalization names where the pattern breaks.
- Field notes sound like field notes: short, earned, specific
  ("constraints placed last are obeyed most").
- Numbers are measurements with sources, never adjectives.

## What the voice never does

- Filler openers ("in today's world", "in this chapter we will").
- AI-tics (validator-banned): delve · tapestry · game-changer ·
  cutting-edge · seamless · "it's important to note".
- Vague verbs where a keystroke exists ("leverage", "utilize" → name
  the command).
- Meta-commentary about being AI-written.
- More than one block claim per paragraph without a receipt nearby.

## Structure (every chapter, this order)

1. Kicker + H1 + lede (lede = outcome promise, ≤40 words).
2. Briefing (≤400 words, time-boxed "5 min", tool-agnostic first).
3. Figures inside the sections they explain (never a gallery at the end).
4. Session: numbered steps, each with goal → paste-block → expected
   terminal → verify → stuck-fix. Minimum 3 steps, maximum 6.
5. Checkpoint (checkbox proofs, persisted via localStorage).
6. Receipts (collapsed `<details>`, rendered from ledger rows only).
7. Pager (prev/next in TOC order).

## Budgets

Prose 1500–2500 words/chapter (code + transcripts excluded from count).
Briefing ≤400 words. Visuals per chapter: 1 signature SVG (geometry
only) + 2–4 HTML infographics, spanning ≥3 different forms — the brief
names the trio. Richness is a number: fewer than 3 visual blocks is a
validator error.

## Freshness (knowledge must be current, never carried on trust)

- Every time-sensitive claim (install commands, model names, free-tier
  limits, harness UI) is re-fetched live during evidence packing and
  stamped with a fetch date in the chapter ("verified 2026-09-XX").
- Ledger rows from August that cannot be re-verified are cut or labeled
  stale — never silently inherited.
- The teach test: each brief states the reader behavior the chapter
  changes. The adversary fails any chapter whose session doesn't produce
  that behavior. No behavior change = no chapter.
- P5 ledger as-of audit: whole-book sweep of receipt dates before
  cutover.
