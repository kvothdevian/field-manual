§ method

Method & guarantees

  • Protocol is law. Six parallel research agents, one row per claim, five columns every time (URL · claim · tier · as-of · fetched). Chapters may append rows while drafting, but each new row follows the same vetting shape. If it is not in this file, it is not cited.
  • Verification before citation. fetched y means a subagent retrieved the page live this wave. Time-sensitive rows restamp when a chapter drafts against them; anything fetched as y (snapshot) carries its transport caveat inline.
  • Tier carries meaning, not decoration. T1 does the arguing, T2 is always flagged as marketing, T3 never stands alone. Cite shape+label (dot + badge) so the argument survives grayscale and print.
  • Coverage proof. The stat strip above is a checksum: 16 chapters + App A, 163 rows, ~135 T1 anchors. The coverage matrix at docs/coverage-matrix.md is the other half — dimensions × chapters — and this ledger is the first half, sources × chapters.
  • Living, but dated. This page is frozen as-of 2026-08-26. Re-run a fetch pass, bump the date, and the diff is the errata.
