# Brief ch15 — "Costs, limits & grey zones" (price it before you touch it)

Status: PROPOSED — batch approval with ch12–ch14/ch16. Frozen on approval.

## Outcome promise

Leave with a written cost/limit dashboard — your own last session
priced from its transcript, a monthly cap with a pre-written stop
rule, and one grey-zone risk card triaged on paper, never executed.

## Coverage dimensions (matrix-bound)

Burst/throughput/budget dials + data price (●) · four-number
reading ritual (●) · distrusted meters (●) · silence-is-citable
(●) · grey-zone arithmetic + death-forecasts (●) · numbers expire
(●). ONE compact reference chapter (DECISIONS #18).

## Venue

Scratch repo `cost-lab/` (`PRICES.md` + `RECEIPT.md` + `RISK-
CARD.md`). Executed: price table from named pricing pages (refuse
any number without a quoted line); reader's own prior transcript
priced by shown arithmetic (hand/`node -e` re-run); cap + halt
sentence; ONE canonical grey-zone exhibit paper-triaged (offer ·
risk · death-forecast · verdict) — no service touched, no spend,
no installs. Book-only.

## Briefing (≤500w)

Stakes (read a rate-limit page like an engineer; cap your worst
month; price a shortcut) → three dials + hidden price (burst RPM
· throughput TPM in/out · budget caps; data/training-rights
price) → reading ritual (RPM/ITPM/OTPM/cap + date stamp; Anthropic
bucket worked example, re-verified row only) → distrusted meters
(shared pools, token credits, vendor averages as order-of-
magnitude, check your own /status) → silence-is-citable
(OpenRouter prints the trade; z.ai usage-policy silent on
training = no opt-out to point at; Gemini/Mistral training claims
stay T3 leads; the workflow IS the lesson) → grey-zone arithmetic
(risk = probability × consequence; stability × legality + death-
forecast; relays/credential-sharing violate written terms; uptime
≠ permission; canonical exhibit de-telegraphed) → breaks-down
(numbers expire — the recheck date is the artifact; $0 stays the
on-ramp).

## Session (4 steps)

1. Price table: rows only from quoted lines; drop + STALE the rest.
2. Price your last session: transcript tokens × row; arithmetic
   shown; hand re-run.
3. Cap + stop rule: trigger AND action named; default = cheapest
   door / halt.
4. Paper-triage ONE exhibit: tier-labeled receipts; verdict
   present; nothing touched; default verdict = walk-away.
